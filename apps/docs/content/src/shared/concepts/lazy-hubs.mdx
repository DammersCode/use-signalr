---
title: Lazy hubs
description: Connect a hub only while a consumer needs it, and keep it open for a grace period.
---

By default, the library connects every hub in the contract when the app starts. A lazy hub connects only when a consumer needs it. Set `lazy: true` for one hub, or for all hubs.

## Grace period

`graceMs` is the time that a lazy hub stays connected after its last consumer leaves. A short grace period prevents a disconnect and a new connect when a component remounts quickly. The default is 0. `graceMs` is a per-hub option.

When the grace period ends, the hub stops and its status is `"idle"`. When a consumer comes back, the hub connects again.

## What counts as a consumer

The library counts the consumers of each hub. The hub is wanted while the count is more than 0. A consumer is not a component. It is one call of a helper that is bound to a hub.

- <Api of="keepAlive" />, which keeps the hub connected and does nothing else.
- <Api of="event" />, <Api of="invoke" />, <Api of="send" />, and <Api of="teardown" />.
- <Api of="status" /> and <Api of="reconnected" />.

</Only>

<Only in="vue">
The consumer ends when the effect scope of the helper stops, for example when the component unmounts.

</Only>
<Only in="angular">
The consumer ends when the injection context is destroyed.

</Only>
<Only in="svelte">
The consumer ends when the component is destroyed.
